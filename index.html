Statement

The limits of language are not the limits of understanding — meaning lives in the spaces between what is spoken and what is heard.

I'm an undergraduate at UESTC studying Software Engineering, and my Chinese name is 刘晋良. They say the best researchers are those who remain students forever — well, I'm still very much a student, ordinary, curious, and constantly realizing how much I don't know.

My research centers on multimodal large language models, with a particular focus on post-training alignment across text, image, and audio. I also explore knowledge-graph retrieval for multi-hop reasoning and tool-augmented agents for planning complex tasks.
