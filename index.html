🔭Research Interests

My research centers on multi-modal large language models, with a particular focus on post-training alignment for generative models across text, image, and audio. Recent work has explored RL and preference optimization for autoregressive multi-modal generation.

I’m also interested in knowledge-graph-based retrieval-augmented generation for multi-hop reasoning, and tool-augmented agents for planning and decomposition of complex tasks. Always happy to discuss any of these — feel free to reach out.
